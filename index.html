Liquidity Buffer Sizing Model

IE 4830 Internship Project, Yaşar University, 2026

A Monte Carlo and newsvendor model that finds the TL liquidity buffer with the lowest expected cost under deposit uncertainty, framed as a hypothetical overnight-carry scenario.

  • Excel
  • Monte Carlo
  • Newsvendor
  • CBRT EVDS
Method and details
  • Built a total TL deposit series from six CBRT EVDS sub-series (53 monthly observations, Jan 2022 to Jun 2026) and computed monthly percentage changes (mean 4.18%, standard deviation 3.22%).
  • Ran a 10,000-scenario Monte Carlo simulation, costing emergency funding at the CBRT overnight lending rate (40%) and idle cash at the overnight borrowing rate (35.5%).
  • Tested buffers from 0 to 2 standard deviations above the mean, averaging 10 recalculations per level. The lowest expected cost was at 0.1σ, and cost rose sharply beyond 0.5σ.
  • Cross-checked with the newsvendor critical ratio (0.40 / (0.40 + 0.355) ≈ 0.53), which also places the optimum near the mean.
  • Stress test: with the emergency rate at 46% (the March 2025 CBRT precedent), the optimal buffer moves to 0.25σ and the critical ratio rises to 56.4%.
  • Limitation: the scenario is hypothetical and assumes normally distributed monthly changes, while the data is slightly right-skewed.

ConvertLab: Unit Converter for Engineers

Claude Code workshop project, 2026

A free browser-based unit converter with dark mode, a USD/TRY FX swap pricer and an OEE calculator, published here on GitHub Pages.

Open live demo

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • GitHub Pages
Method and details
  • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. All calculations run in the browser, with no server.
  • Converts length, mass, pressure, energy and temperature. Temperature goes through Kelvin because it is not a simple multiplication.
  • Dark mode starts from the device theme and remembers the user's choice.
  • FX swap tab: theoretical USD/TRY forward from covered interest parity, F = S × (1 + rTRY × d/365) / (1 + rUSD × d/360), with swap points and the annualized premium. Checked against exact-fraction reference values and a round-trip test. Illustrative inputs, not a dealing quote.
  • OEE tab: Availability × Performance × Quality for a production shift, with a plain-language reading of the biggest loss. Checked against exact-fraction reference values, and against the identity OEE = good units × ideal time / planned time on 2,000 random cases.
  • Built during a Claude Code workshop. The pricing section on the demo is a mock-up, not a real product.
